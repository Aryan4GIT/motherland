One supply chain, three ways in

Motherland Group is a B2B and B2C apparel solution provider. We manufacture denim and denim accessories, add value with garment washing, sell factory-fresh garments at retail, and customise, alter and refashion denim one pair at a time.

For exporters and brands

Motherland Garments

Four units across Bangalore and Chennai serving almost every leading garment exporter and domestic brand. From 1000 garments a day in 1995 to a 60,000-a-day capacity.

For brands and individuals

mDenim

Not only your suits and coats can be tailored — your denim can too, in a variety of designs and washes. We also repair, alter and refashion old denim professionally.

mSquare

Five stores across Bangalore stocked with factory-fresh denims, chinos, casual shirts, knits, tees, tracks, joggers, kids and ladies wear in the latest fabrics, fits and washes.
