Factory fresh,
five stores, one city

Motherland Group, with its strong presence in garment manufacturing, is also into garment retail under the brand mSquare — with the expertise of working with top Indian and international brands across menswear, womenswear, kids and babies.

Retail

mSquare stores sit at prime locations across Bangalore. In the first year of operations there are already five, well stocked with apparel for every occasion — formals, semi-casuals and casuals.

Wholesale

mSquare also runs a B2B model, offering garments to other retailers with stock in every category at attractive prices.

A lot has changed since 1995. With over 25 years of a customer-focused approach and a continuous quest for quality, Motherland Group today processes over 60,000 garments a day and has diversified into many garment-based B2B and B2C solutions.
