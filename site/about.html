KNOW MORE ABOUT US

They worked hard with missionary zeal

In 1995 three young textile chemistry graduates, along with a mechanical engineer, started a small garment processing unit in a makeshift shed with a capacity of 1000 garments per day. They nurtured the fledgling unit into a reliable supplier.

With sheer hard work and commitment the business started to make a name for itself in Chennai. A lot has changed since. With over 25 years of a customer-focused approach and a continuous quest for quality, Motherland Group today processes over 60,000 garments a day and has diversified into many garment-based B2B and B2C solutions.

OUR HISTORY

Thirty years,
one line at a time

From one loaned machine in Guindy to four units, a denim-only washing plant and a retail chain. Drag the line to move through it.

1995

The journey begins in a makeshift shed in Chennai. Jaihind Garments: one set of machinery on loan, one customer, 500 shirts a day.

1997

A sick unit is taken over and starts serving customers in the Ambattur area under the name Jaihind Textile Processors.

1998

Jaihind Garments moves from Guindy to its own premises at SIDCO Thirumudivakkam.

2005

The milestone year. A big facility is taken over at Magadi Road, Bangalore. The existing companies merge and are named Motherland Garments Private Limited.

2012

Second unit opens at KIADB Industrial Area, Kanakapura Road, Harohalli & Peenya, Bangalore.

2016

A new venture, mDenim, for alteration, refashion and customisation of denims.

2020

Third unit, exclusive for denim washing, at Peenya. mSquare, the retail arm of Motherland Group, opens on Magadi Road and in Peenya.

Vision

To be the best apparel solution provider to garment manufacturers, brands, exporters and retail customers — with the least impact on the planet.

Mission

  • Provide a wide variety of garment solutions to garment exporters, brand manufacturers and retail customers.
  • Help exporters, brands and manufacturers stay above their competition with sustainable garment washing and manufacturing services.
  • Help retail customers get international quality and value for money with a unique product line and denim customisation.
